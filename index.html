Who We Are ?

We are team of Binayaka Abasika Bidyala, seeking to help every child learn, grow and succeed in life. We provide quality education helping children gain not only academic knowledge but also English communication skills, creativity, confidence, discipline and critical thinking.

Since every child has unique talents and abilities, we understand them. Through supportive teachers, practical learning activities, sports, and cultural programs, we encourage children to discover their strengths and develop good values.
